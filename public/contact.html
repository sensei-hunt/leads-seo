Common Reasons to Contact Us

  • Account access issues — locked accounts, forgotten passwords, trouble signing in
  • FSA balance questions — checking your remaining balance or contribution history
  • Claims support — submitting claims, checking claim status, or disputing a denial
  • Eligible expenses — confirming whether a specific item or service qualifies for FSA reimbursement
  • Enrollment changes — adjusting contributions after a qualifying life event
  • FSA card issues — reporting a lost or stolen debit card, requesting a replacement
  • Tax documents — requesting copies of FSA contribution statements

For the fastest resolution, sign in to your account and submit a support request. Our team can access your account details directly and resolve most issues within one business day.

Before You Call

To help us assist you quickly, please have the following information ready:

  • Your full name as it appears on your benefits enrollment
  • Your employer name
  • The email address associated with your account
  • A brief description of your question or issue

Many common questions can be answered immediately — check our Frequently Asked Questions page before reaching out.
