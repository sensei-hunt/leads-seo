Online Security – Protecting Your Account

At Alliance Insurance Group, safeguarding your personal and financial information is our top priority. We employ industry-leading security measures to protect your data, and we encourage you to follow best practices to keep your account secure.

How We Protect You

Your information is protected by multiple layers of security technology and protocols:

  • 256-bit SSL Encryption: All data transmitted between your browser and our servers is encrypted using industry-standard TLS/SSL encryption, ensuring your information cannot be intercepted.
  • Multi-Factor Authentication: We offer multi-factor authentication (MFA) to add an extra layer of verification when you sign in, beyond just your password.
  • Automatic Session Timeout: Your session will automatically end after a period of inactivity to prevent unauthorized access if you step away from your device.
  • Secure Data Storage: All personal and financial data is stored in encrypted, access-controlled environments that meet or exceed industry compliance standards.
  • Continuous Monitoring: Our security team monitors for suspicious activity around the clock and employs intrusion detection systems to identify potential threats.
  • Regular Security Audits: We conduct periodic security assessments and penetration testing to identify and address vulnerabilities.

Protecting Your Account

While we work hard to protect your information on our end, account security is a shared responsibility. Follow these tips to help keep your account safe:

Use a strong, unique password. Choose a password that is at least 12 characters long and includes a mix of uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and special characters. Never reuse passwords from other websites.
Enable multi-factor authentication. When available, always turn on MFA for your account. This adds a second verification step — such as a code sent to your phone — making it much harder for unauthorized users to access your account.
Don't share your login credentials. Never share your username, password, or verification codes with anyone, including people claiming to be from Alliance Insurance Group. Our staff will never ask for your password.
Sign in from trusted devices and networks. Avoid accessing your account from public computers or unsecured Wi-Fi networks. If you must use a public network, use a VPN for added protection.
Keep your software up to date. Ensure your web browser, operating system, and antivirus software are updated to the latest versions to protect against known security vulnerabilities.

Recognizing Phishing Attempts

Phishing is a common method used by criminals to steal personal information. Be cautious of:

  • Emails or texts that create a sense of urgency ("Your account will be locked!")
  • Messages asking you to click a link to verify your account or update your information
  • Communications from email addresses that don't match our official domain
  • Requests for your password, Social Security number, or other sensitive information
Important: Alliance Insurance Group will never send you an unsolicited email asking for your password, Social Security number, or full account details. If you receive a suspicious message, do not click any links. Forward it to fsa@allianceinsgroup.com and then delete it.

What to Do If You Suspect Unauthorized Activity

If you notice any unfamiliar transactions, login attempts, or changes to your account that you didn't make, take these steps immediately:

  • Change your password right away
  • Contact our support team at 866-396-3967
  • Review your recent account activity for any unauthorized transactions
  • If you believe your identity has been compromised, consider placing a fraud alert with the major credit bureaus

Want to update your security settings? Sign in to your account to enable multi-factor authentication and review your security preferences.
