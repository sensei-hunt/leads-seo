Alliance Insurance Group – Employee Benefits & FSA Portal

Welcome to the official Alliance Insurance Group employee benefits portal. Sign in to manage your Flexible Spending Account (FSA), shop for FSA-eligible health items, access benefits documents and forms, and calculate your tax savings — all in one place.

Use this portal to check your FSA balance, submit reimbursement claims, browse eligible expenses, review plan documents, and take advantage of pre-tax savings on healthcare, dental, and vision expenses. Whether you need to find eligible items, download a claim form, or estimate your tax savings, everything is available after you sign in.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I log in to my Alliance Insurance Group benefits account?

Go to aigwealthcareportalweb.com and click Sign In. Enter your username and password to access your FSA benefits, view eligible expenses, and manage your account.

What is a Flexible Spending Account (FSA)?

An FSA is a pre-tax benefit account that lets you set aside money from your paycheck before taxes to pay for eligible healthcare expenses. This reduces your taxable income and saves you money on medical, dental, and vision costs.

What can I buy with my FSA?

FSA funds can be used for a wide range of eligible healthcare items including prescriptions, over-the-counter medications, first aid supplies, sunscreen, thermometers, blood pressure monitors, knee braces, TENS units, and more. Use the FSA Store search tool on our portal to check eligibility.

How do I check my FSA balance?

Sign in to your account at aigwealthcareportalweb.com to view your current FSA balance, recent transactions, and remaining eligible expenses for the plan year.

What happens to unused FSA funds at the end of the year?

FSA plans typically follow a use-it-or-lose-it rule, meaning unused funds may be forfeited at the end of the plan year. However, some plans offer a grace period or allow a small rollover amount. Check your specific plan details for more information.

How do I submit an FSA reimbursement claim?

Log in to your account, navigate to the claims section, and submit your eligible expense with a receipt or explanation of benefits (EOB). You can also use your FSA debit card at the point of sale for automatic processing.

Always confirm you are on aigwealthcareportalweb.com before entering your username or password.
