Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about your Flexible Spending Account and the Alliance Insurance Group benefits portal. Can't find what you're looking for? Contact our support team.

Account & Enrollment

What is a Flexible Spending Account (FSA)?

An FSA is a tax-advantaged account that allows you to set aside pre-tax dollars from your paycheck to pay for eligible healthcare expenses such as copays, prescriptions, dental care, vision care, and many over-the-counter items. Because contributions are made before taxes, you save money on every dollar you put in.

How do I enroll in an FSA?

You can enroll during your employer's annual open enrollment period. If you experience a qualifying life event — such as marriage, the birth of a child, or a change in employment — you may also enroll or make changes within 30 days of that event. Sign in to your account to complete enrollment online, or contact your HR department for assistance.

How much can I contribute to my FSA?

For the 2026 plan year, the IRS maximum contribution for a Healthcare FSA is $3,050. Dependent Care FSA limits are $5,000 per household (or $2,500 if married and filing separately). Your employer may set a lower maximum — check your plan details for specifics.

Can I change my FSA contribution amount mid-year?

Generally, FSA elections are fixed for the plan year. However, you may be able to adjust your contribution if you experience a qualifying life event such as marriage, divorce, birth or adoption of a child, or a change in employment status.

Managing Your Account

How do I check my FSA balance?

Sign in to your account to view your current balance, year-to-date contributions, pending claims, and transaction history. Your balance is updated in real time after each processed transaction.

How do I submit an FSA claim?

Sign in to your account and navigate to the Claims section. Click "Submit New Claim," enter the expense details, and upload a copy of your receipt or Explanation of Benefits (EOB). Claims are typically processed within 3-5 business days. You'll receive an email notification when your claim is approved.

I forgot my password. How do I reset it?

On the sign-in page, click "Forgot Password" and enter the email address associated with your account. You'll receive a password reset link within a few minutes. If you don't see the email, check your spam folder or contact support for assistance.

What happens to unused FSA funds at the end of the year?

FSA funds are generally subject to a "use-it-or-lose-it" policy, meaning unused funds may be forfeited at the end of the plan year. However, many employers offer either a grace period (up to 2.5 additional months to spend remaining funds) or a carryover provision (up to $610 can roll into the next year). Check your specific plan details or ask your HR department which option applies.

Eligible Expenses

What can I buy with my FSA?

FSA funds cover a wide range of medical, dental, vision, and over-the-counter expenses. Common eligible items include doctor visit copays, prescriptions, dental cleanings, eyeglasses, contact lenses, sunscreen, first aid supplies, and menstrual care products. Visit our complete eligible expenses list for details.

Are over-the-counter medications FSA eligible?

Yes. Since the CARES Act of 2020, over-the-counter medications and menstrual care products are FSA eligible without a prescription. This includes pain relievers, allergy medications, cold remedies, digestive aids, and more.

Can I use my FSA for dental and vision expenses?

Yes. Dental expenses such as cleanings, fillings, orthodontia, and crowns are eligible. Vision expenses including eye exams, prescription glasses, contact lenses, and LASIK surgery are also covered. See our eligible expenses page for the full list.
