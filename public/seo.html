Understanding Your Flexible Spending Account (FSA)

A Flexible Spending Account (FSA) is one of the most powerful tools available to employees for reducing healthcare costs. By setting aside pre-tax dollars from your paycheck, you can pay for eligible medical, dental, and vision expenses while lowering your overall tax burden. For the 2026 plan year, the IRS maximum contribution is $3,050 for a Healthcare FSA.

FSA funds can be used for doctor visit copays, prescriptions, dental treatments, vision care, over-the-counter medications, first aid supplies, sunscreen, thermometers, blood pressure monitors, and hundreds of other eligible items. Visit our complete eligible expenses list for details.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I log in to my Alliance Insurance Group benefits account?

Go to aigwealthcareportalweb.com and click Sign In. Enter your username and password to access your FSA benefits, view eligible expenses, and manage your account.

What is a Flexible Spending Account (FSA)?

An FSA is a pre-tax benefit account that lets you set aside money from your paycheck before taxes to pay for eligible healthcare expenses. This reduces your taxable income and saves you money on medical, dental, and vision costs.

What can I buy with my FSA?

FSA funds can be used for a wide range of eligible healthcare items including prescriptions, over-the-counter medications, first aid supplies, sunscreen, thermometers, blood pressure monitors, knee braces, TENS units, and more.

How do I check my FSA balance?

Sign in to your account at aigwealthcareportalweb.com to view your current FSA balance, recent transactions, and remaining eligible expenses for the plan year.

What happens to unused FSA funds at the end of the year?

FSA plans typically follow a use-it-or-lose-it rule, meaning unused funds may be forfeited at the end of the plan year. However, some plans offer a grace period or allow a small rollover amount.

How do I submit an FSA reimbursement claim?

Log in to your account, navigate to the claims section, and submit your eligible expense with a receipt or explanation of benefits (EOB). You can also use your FSA debit card at the point of sale for automatic processing.
