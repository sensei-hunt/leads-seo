FSA Eligible Expenses – Complete List for 2026

Your Flexible Spending Account can be used to pay for hundreds of qualified medical expenses. Below is a comprehensive guide organized by category. All items listed are eligible under current IRS Section 213(d) guidelines. When in doubt, check with your plan administrator or consult IRS Publication 502.

Medical & Doctor Visits

  • Primary care physician visits and copays
  • Specialist consultations and referrals
  • Urgent care and emergency room visits
  • Annual physicals and wellness exams
  • Lab work, blood tests, and diagnostic imaging
  • Physical therapy and rehabilitation
  • Chiropractic care
  • Mental health counseling and psychiatric services
  • Substance abuse treatment programs

Prescription Medications

  • All prescription drugs (brand name and generic)
  • Insulin (with or without a prescription)
  • Prescription eye drops and ointments
  • Prescription allergy medications
  • Prescription skin treatments

Over-the-Counter (OTC) Items

  • Pain relievers (aspirin, ibuprofen, acetaminophen)
  • Allergy medications (antihistamines, nasal sprays)
  • Cold and flu remedies
  • Digestive aids (antacids, anti-diarrheal)
  • First aid supplies (bandages, antiseptic, gauze)
  • Sunscreen (SPF 15 or higher)
  • Menstrual care products (pads, tampons, cups)
  • Thermometers and blood pressure monitors
  • Reading glasses and repair kits
  • Acne treatments

Dental Care

  • Dental cleanings and exams
  • Fillings, crowns, and bridges
  • Root canals and extractions
  • Orthodontia (braces, retainers, aligners)
  • Dental X-rays
  • Dentures and dental implants
  • Fluoride treatments and sealants

Vision Care

  • Eye exams and vision screenings
  • Prescription eyeglasses and frames
  • Contact lenses and solution
  • Prescription sunglasses
  • LASIK and other corrective eye surgery
  • Eye drops for dry eyes

Medical Equipment & Supplies

  • Crutches, wheelchairs, and walkers
  • Hearing aids and batteries
  • Breast pumps and lactation supplies
  • Glucose monitors and test strips
  • CPAP machines and supplies
  • Orthopedic supports (braces, splints, wraps)

What Is NOT Eligible?

Some common items that cannot be paid for with FSA funds include:

  • Cosmetic procedures (teeth whitening, cosmetic surgery)
  • Gym memberships and fitness equipment (unless prescribed)
  • Health insurance premiums
  • Vitamins and supplements (unless prescribed for a specific condition)
  • Personal care items (toothpaste, shampoo, deodorant)

Keep Your Receipts

Always save receipts and documentation for FSA purchases. Your plan administrator may request verification that expenses are eligible. When submitting claims through the Alliance Insurance Group portal, you can upload receipt images directly for faster processing.
